in-memory simulation implementing the same API. Everything about the pages themselves
(rendering, forms, validation, filtering, ordering, live updates, RTL, mobile layout)
was genuinely exercised; the Firebase round-trip and the security rules themselves
were NOT executed against the real service (see "Not verified" below).
